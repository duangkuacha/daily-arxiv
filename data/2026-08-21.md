Method: 在Asplund空间中发展系统的变分分析框架，建立方向性次微分演算规则；针对无穷维空间有界集非紧致的问题，引入一个新的方向性条件，基于该条件推导值函数方向极限次微分和奇异次微分的上界估计。

Result: 成功将方向性非光滑分析推广到无穷维空间，建立了必要的方向性演算规则，并在新引入的方向性条件下获得了值函数方向次微分的上界估计。

Conclusion: 该研究为无穷维分层系统的灵敏度分析提供了更精细的分析基础，填补了方向次微分理论从有限维到无穷维推广的空白。

Abstract: Directional subdifferentials of the value function provide a quantitative measure of optimal value response to perturbations. While existing results are largely limited to finite-dimensional settings, this paper develops a comprehensive variational framework in Asplund spaces. We establish essential directional calculus rules, extending directional nonsmooth analysis to infinite dimensions. To address the lack of compactness of bounded sets in infinite-dimensional spaces, we introduce a new directional condition, under which we derive upper estimates for directional limiting and singular subdifferentials of the value function. These results provide a refined analytical foundation for sensitivity analysis in infinite-dimensional hierarchical systems.
