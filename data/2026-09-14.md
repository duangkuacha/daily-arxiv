Method: 利用已有单边抛物型障碍问题的可微性结果，并证明一个新的关于非扩张参数化不动点方程解算子方向可微性的定理，从而得到双边问题解映射的方向可微性。

Result: 首次建立了带双侧全分布逐点不等式约束的抛物型演化变分不等式解映射的方向可微性。

Conclusion: 所获可微性结果可用于推导双边抛物型障碍问题最优控制问题的最优性条件；其中关于非扩张参数化不动点方程的新定理也可能有其它应用价值。

Abstract: We prove that the solution map of the classical bilateral parabolic obstacle problem is directionally differentiable when interpreted as a function between suitable Lebesgue and Sobolev spaces. To the best of our knowledge, this paper is the first to establish this kind of differentiability for a parabolic evolution variational inequality with two-sided fully distributed pointwise inequality constraints. Our analysis relies on known differentiability results for unilateral parabolic obstacle problems and a new theorem on the directional differentiability of solution operators of non-expansive parameterized fixed-point equations. The latter may also be of interest for other applications. Our results can be used, for example, to derive optimality conditions for optimal control problems governed by bilateral parabolic obstacle problems.
