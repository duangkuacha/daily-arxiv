Method: 论文采用李括号分析的方法：一方面证明一个新的可控性充分条件，该条件能同时利用更多"好"的四次李括号；另一方面识别一类可控性阻碍，这些阻碍依赖于第二类坐标是控制的二次型正定泛函的李括号。

Result: 论文给出了新的充分条件和阻碍条件两方面的结果，证明了两者的互补性，并以此回应了Kawski 1987年的开放问题；同时提供了示例和评注来阐明这些概念的微妙之处。

Conclusion: 四次李括号在标量输入系统的小时间局部可控性中扮演重要角色，好与坏的四次括号之间可以通过构造合适的Hall基来区分，从而为这一经典问题提供了新的解答。

Abstract: We investigate the role of quartic terms in the small-time local controllability of scalar-input systems. First, we prove a new sufficient condition for controllability, which exploits simultaneously more good quartic Lie brackets than previous results. Second, we identify a family of quartic obstructions to controllability, relying on Lie brackets whose coordinates of the second kind are positive-definite functionals of the control. Third, we show that the complementarity of these results can be seen as an answer to Kawski's 1987 open problem concerning the construction of a Hall basis which somehow separates good and bad quartic brackets. We give examples and remarks illustrating some of the intricacies of these notions.
