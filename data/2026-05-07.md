Motivation: 探究非交换性度量μ_p与经典相依函数之间的内在联系，特别是明确哪些相依度量能捕捉非交换性信息，以及非交换性与相依性之间的定量关系。

Method: 通过理论分析，研究对称化变换对Spearman's ρ的影响；分析Blomqvist's β与非交换性的关系；推导σ与μ_1之间的下界不等式；并针对最大非交换性族{M_θ}，推导Kendall's τ、Spearman's ρ及尾部相依系数的闭式表达式。

Result: 1) 对称化C→(C+C^t)/2保持Spearman's ρ不变，但使μ_p为零；2) Blomqvist's β不包含非交换性信息；3) 建立了σ(C)≥6μ_1(C)的严格下界，表明非交换性必然蕴含相依性；4) 给出了最大非交换性族{M_θ}的τ、ρ和尾部相依系数的闭式表达式。

Conclusion: 非交换性与相依性之间存在定量关联，但并非所有经典相依函数都能捕获非交换性信息；不对称性可以作为一种依赖性的度量。

Abstract: We study the relationship between measures of non-exchangeability $μ_p$ ($p\in[1,+\infty]$), in the sense of Durante et al. (2010), and classical dependence functionals for bivariate copulas. We show that the symmetrization $C\mapsto(C+C^t)/2$ preserves Spearman's $ρ$ while annihilating $μ_p$, and that Blomqvist's $β$ carries no information about the degree of non-exchangeability. We also establish the sharp lower bound $σ(C)\ge 6\,μ_1(C)$, where $σ$ is the Schweizer-Wolff dependence measure, showing that asymmetry implies dependence. Closed-form expressions for $τ$, $ρ$, and the tail-dependence coefficients of the maximally non-exchangeable family $\{M_θ\}$ are derived as illustrations.
